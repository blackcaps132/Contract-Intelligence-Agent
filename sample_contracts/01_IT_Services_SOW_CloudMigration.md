# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 01: CLOUD MIGRATION SERVICES

**Client: Contoso Tech Solutions LLC | Provider: Apex Cloud Consulting Inc.**

---

## RECITALS AND PARTIES

This Statement of Work No. 01 ('SOW') is entered into as of October 15, 2026 ('Effective Date'), by and between Contoso Tech Solutions LLC ('Client'), a Delaware limited liability company, and Apex Cloud Consulting Inc. ('Service Provider'), a Washington corporation.

This SOW is governed by and subject to the terms and conditions of the Master Services Agreement entered into between the Parties. In the event of any conflict, this SOW shall govern with respect to the specific project deliverables outlined herein.

## SECTION 1. SCOPE OF WORK AND DELIVERABLES

1.1 Project Overview: Service Provider shall deliver professional enterprise cloud architecture, containerization, and automated deployment services for Client's core transaction processing application.

1.2 Deliverables Breakdown:

• Phase 1 Deliverable: Cloud Architecture Blueprint and Security Architecture Document detailing target AWS/Azure infrastructure, virtual private networks, IAM policies, and disaster recovery specifications.

• Phase 2 Deliverable: Docker containerization of core microservices, Helm charts, and Kubernetes cluster configuration with automated node scaling.

• Phase 3 Deliverable: Production CI/CD automated deployment pipelines utilizing GitHub Actions, automated smoke testing, and successful production cutover.

## SECTION 2. MILESTONE SCHEDULE AND ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

2.1 Project Milestones: Phase 1 shall be completed by November 30, 2026. Phase 2 shall be completed by January 15, 2027. Phase 3 shall be completed by March 31, 2027 ('Target Completion Date').

2.2 Review and Acceptance: Client shall have ten (10) business days from delivery of each milestone deliverable to review and perform acceptance testing. Deliverables shall be deemed accepted unless Client delivers written notice specifying non-conformance within such period.

## SECTION 3. PERSONNEL AND HOURLY RATE SCHEDULE

3.1 Staffing and Hourly Rates: Service Provider shall provide qualified technical personnel at the following agreed hourly rates:

• Solutions Architect: $145.00 per hour

• Senior Cloud Developer: $95.00 per hour

• DevOps / CI-CD Specialist: $110.00 per hour

• QA Automation Engineer: $80.00 per hour

3.2 No Rate Changes: The hourly rates specified above shall remain firm and fixed for the entire duration of this SOW unless modified by mutual written amendment.

## SECTION 4. BUDGET CEILING AND NOT-TO-EXCEED (NTE) CAP

4.1 Budget Ceiling: The total cumulative fees payable by Client for all services and deliverables under this SOW shall not exceed $150,000.00 USD ('Not-to-Exceed Budget Cap').

4.2 Cap Enforcement: Service Provider shall not perform services nor bill hours that would cause aggregate billings to exceed the Not-to-Exceed Budget Cap without prior written approval from Client's authorized representative.

## SECTION 5. INVOICING, PAYMENT TERMS AND DISCOUNTS

5.1 Invoicing Frequency: Invoices shall be submitted bi-weekly based upon actual hours worked and milestone completion.

5.2 Payment Terms: All undisputed invoice amounts are due and payable Net thirty (30) days from the invoice date.

5.3 Prompt Payment Discount: Client shall be entitled to deduct a two percent (2.0%) prompt payment discount from the gross invoiced amount if payment is made within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date.

5.4 Late Interest: Undisputed amounts overdue beyond thirty (30) days shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum statutory rate permitted by law.

6.1 Work-Made-for-Hire: All code, scripts, architectural designs, documentation, and work product developed specifically for Client under this SOW shall constitute 'Work-Made-for-Hire'.

6.2 Assignment: To the extent any deliverable does not qualify as work-made-for-hire, Service Provider hereby unconditionally assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to such deliverables upon payment.

## SECTION 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this SOW at any time, with or without cause, upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party.

7.2 Effect of Termination: Client shall pay for hours verified and deliverables accepted up to the effective termination date.
