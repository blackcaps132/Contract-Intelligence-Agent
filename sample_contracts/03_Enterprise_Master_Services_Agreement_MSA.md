# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Customer: Global FinTech Dynamics Corp | Provider: Meridian Enterprise Solutions Inc.**

---

## PREAMBLE AND PURPOSE

This Master Services Agreement ('Agreement') is made effective as of September 1, 2026, by and between Global FinTech Dynamics Corp ('Customer'), a Delaware corporation, and Meridian Enterprise Solutions Inc. ('Provider'), a California corporation.

## SECTION 1. SERVICES AND STATEMENTS OF WORK

1.1 SOW Execution: From time to time, the parties may execute Statements of Work ('SOW') referencing this Agreement. Each SOW shall constitute a separate contract incorporating all terms herein.

## SECTION 2. MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY

2.1 Confidential Information: Each party agrees to safeguard the other party's confidential and proprietary information using the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential data, but no less than reasonable care.

2.2 Survival: Confidentiality obligations under this Section shall survive for a period of three (3) years following the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## SECTION 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

3.1 Customer Work Product: Provider agrees that all custom work product developed specifically for Customer under any SOW shall be owned exclusively by Customer as work-made-for-hire upon payment.

3.2 Provider Background IP: Provider retains sole ownership of its pre-existing tools, libraries, and frameworks, granting Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive license to utilize such Background IP embedded in deliverables.

## SECTION 4. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION

4.1 Provider Indemnity: Provider shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer against any third-party claims, suits, or proceedings alleging that the deliverables infringe any patent, copyright, or trademark.

4.2 Customer Indemnity: Customer shall defend and indemnify Provider against any claims alleging that materials supplied by Customer violate third-party rights or applicable laws.

## SECTION 5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

5.1 Aggregate Liability Cap: In no event shall either party's aggregate cumulative liability arising out of or related to this Agreement exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the incident giving rise to liability.

5.2 Consequential Damages Waiver: Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, except for breaches of confidentiality or gross negligence.

## SECTION 6. TERM, TERMINATION AND GOVERNING LAW

6.1 Term: This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years.

6.2 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon sixty (60) days prior written notice.

6.3 Governing Law and Venue: This Agreement shall be governed by Delaware law. Any disputes shall be resolved in state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.
