# DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY PROCESSING ADDENDUM (DPA)

**Controller: Acme Global E-Commerce LLC | Processor: SwiftData Cloud Storage Ltd.**

---

## PURPOSE AND ROLES

This Data Processing Addendum ('DPA') supplements the Master Agreement between Acme Global E-Commerce LLC ('Data Controller') and SwiftData Cloud Storage Ltd. ('Data Processor') effective as of July 1, 2026.

This DPA governs the processing of personal data in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

## SECTION 1. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS AND INSTRUCTIONS

1.1 Documented Instructions: Processor shall process personal data solely on documented instructions from Controller and not for any secondary commercial purposes.

1.2 Confidentiality: Processor ensures that all personnel authorized to process personal data have committed to strict confidentiality.

## SECTION 2. SECURITY INCIDENT AND 72-HOUR BREACH NOTIFICATION

2.1 Technical Safeguards: Processor shall implement industry-standard encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), continuous vulnerability monitoring, and role-based access control.

2.2 Breach Notification Window: Processor shall notify Controller in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, upon becoming aware of any confirmed or suspected personal data breach.

## SECTION 3. SUB-PROCESSORS AND AUDIT RIGHTS

3.1 Sub-Processor Notification: Processor shall provide Controller at least thirty (30) days prior written notice before onboarding any new sub-processor, giving Controller the right to object.

3.2 Annual Audit Rights: Processor shall allow Controller or an independent third-party auditor to conduct an annual security inspection and review Processor's current SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications.

## SECTION 4. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS AND RETURN OF DATA

4.1 Data Subject Assistance: Processor shall assist Controller in responding to data subject access, rectification, or deletion requests within five (5) business days of notice.

4.2 Deletion Upon Termination: Upon termination of services, Processor shall delete or securely return all customer personal data within thirty (30) days and provide written certification of destruction.

## SECTION 5. GOVERNING LAW

5.1 Jurisdiction: This DPA shall be governed by the laws of Ireland (for GDPR compliance) or the State of California (for US domestic data).
