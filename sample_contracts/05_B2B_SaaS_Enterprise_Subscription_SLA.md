# ENTERPRISE SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT & SLA

**Customer: NexaHealth Systems Inc. | Provider: CloudPulse Technologies Inc.**

---

## RECITALS AND SUBSCRIPTION GRANT

This Cloud Software as a Service Agreement ('Agreement') is entered into on October 1, 2026, by and between NexaHealth Systems Inc. ('Customer') and CloudPulse Technologies Inc. ('Provider').

Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable enterprise subscription to access and use the CloudPulse Clinical Analytics Platform for up to 500 named user seats.

## SECTION 1. SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND BILLING

1.1 Annual Fee: Customer shall pay an annual enterprise subscription fee of $75,000.00 USD, payable annually in advance within Net thirty (30) days of invoice.

## SECTION 2. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) AND UPTIME

2.1 Monthly Uptime Commitment: Provider guarantees that the SaaS Platform will achieve 99.9% Monthly Uptime Percentage, excluding scheduled maintenance.

2.2 Service Credit Schedule: If Monthly Uptime falls below 99.9%, Customer is entitled to the following SLA Service Credits against future renewals:

• 99.0% to 99.8% Uptime: 10% credit of the monthly prorated subscription fee

• 95.0% to 98.9% Uptime: 25% credit of the monthly prorated subscription fee

• Below 95.0% Uptime: 50% credit of the monthly prorated subscription fee, plus Customer's immediate right to terminate for cause with full prorated refund.

## SECTION 3. DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY COMPLIANCE

3.1 HIPAA and SOC 2: Provider shall maintain SOC 2 Type II compliance and execute a Business Associate Agreement ('BAA') safeguarding all protected patient health information (PHI).

## SECTION 4. TERM, RENEWAL AND NOTICE DEADLINE

4.1 Initial Term and Renewal: The initial term is one (1) year. The subscription shall automatically renew for additional one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the current term.

## SECTION 5. LIABILITY AND JURISDICTION

5.1 Liability Cap: Each party's aggregate liability shall be capped at the total subscription fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

5.2 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
