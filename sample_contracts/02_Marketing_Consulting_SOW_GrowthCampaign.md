# STATEMENT OF WORK: DIGITAL MARKETING GROWTH SERVICES

**Client: BrightWave Retail Inc. | Agency: Horizon Media Agency LLC**

---

## RECITALS AND PARTIES

This Statement of Work for Marketing Growth Services ('SOW') is entered into on November 1, 2026, by and between BrightWave Retail Inc. ('Client') and Horizon Media Agency LLC ('Agency').

## SECTION 1. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES

1.1 Marketing Scope: Agency shall manage paid social advertising channels, conduct digital influencer outreach campaigns, and produce weekly creative video and graphic assets for Client's upcoming seasonal sales campaigns.

## SECTION 2. AGENCY STAFFING AND RATES

2.1 Rate Card: Agency shall provide marketing personnel at the following billable rates:

• Senior Growth Strategist: $125.00 per hour

• Creative Content Designer: $90.00 per hour

• Digital Copywriter: $75.00 per hour

## SECTION 3. INVOICING AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Monthly Invoicing: Invoices shall be submitted monthly for all hours expended by Agency personnel during the preceding month. Invoices are due Net thirty (30) days from receipt.

3.2 No Cap: Client shall be billed for all actual hours expended by Agency personnel on a time-and-materials basis. No maximum fee, budget cap, or not-to-exceed limitation applies to this engagement.

## SECTION 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 Ownership of Ad Creatives: Client shall own all final published creative assets upon receipt of full payment. Agency retains ownership of underlying proprietary marketing frameworks and toolsets.

## SECTION 5. TERMINATION AND JURISDICTION

5.1 Termination: Either party may terminate this agreement upon fifteen (15) days written notice.

5.2 Governing Law: This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
