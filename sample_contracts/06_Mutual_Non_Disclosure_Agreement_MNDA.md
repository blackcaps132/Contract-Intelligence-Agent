# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**Party A: Quantum AI Labs Inc. | Party B: Vertex Robotics Inc.**

---

## PARTIES AND PREAMBLE

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ('Agreement') is made effective as of November 10, 2026, by and between Quantum AI Labs Inc. ('Party A') and Vertex Robotics Inc. ('Party B') to evaluate a potential strategic commercial partnership.

## SECTION 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1 Scope: 'Confidential Information' refers to all proprietary technical, financial, operational, source code, and business data disclosed by either party, whether disclosed orally, in writing, or electronically, marked as confidential or that should reasonably be understood to be confidential.

## SECTION 2. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

2.1 Carve-Outs: Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach; (b) was already in the receiving party's possession prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed without reference to the disclosing party's data; or (d) is required to be disclosed by judicial order.

## SECTION 3. OBLIGATIONS AND DUTY OF CARE

3.1 Protection Standard: Each party agrees to protect the other's confidential data using the same standard of care it uses for its own confidential assets, but in no event less than reasonable care.

3.2 Restricted Access: Access shall be limited strictly to employees and contractors with a valid need-to-know who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement.

## SECTION 4. DURATION AND SURVIVAL TERM

4.1 Term: This Agreement shall govern disclosures made for one (1) year from the Effective Date.

4.2 Survival: The confidentiality obligations regarding disclosed information shall survive for a period of three (3) years from the date of disclosure.

## SECTION 5. RETURN OF MATERIALS AND JURISDICTION

5.1 Return or Destruction: Within ten (10) business days following written request, the receiving party shall return or destroy all copies of confidential data.

5.2 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed under the laws of the State of New York.
